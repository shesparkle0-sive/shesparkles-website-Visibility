Privacy Notice

How SheSparkles Business Services handles information submitted through this website.

Draft notice for review. This is a practical starting point, not a legal opinion or confirmation that all POPIA compliance obligations have been met. Before launch, the business must confirm its responsible party details, Information Officer arrangements, retention periods, security measures and any applicable operator agreements.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

SheSparkles Business Services is responsible for handling personal information submitted through this website for the purposes described in this notice. Contact: shesparkle0@gmail.com or 074 405 5701.

2. What information do we collect?

If you submit an enquiry, we may collect your name, business name, email address, telephone number, the service you are interested in and the message you provide. Please do not submit identity numbers, bank account details, passwords, payslips, employee records, health information or other sensitive/confidential documents through the general enquiry form.

3. Why do we use it?

We use the information to receive, assess and respond to your enquiry, communicate with you about requested services, and keep a basic record of business enquiries. We do not ask you to provide more information than is reasonably needed for these purposes.

4. Form processing and service providers

The website form is configured to use FormSubmit to transmit your submission to SheSparkles by email. This means information entered into the form is handled by that external service as part of delivering the message. FormSubmit's own privacy terms and security practices also apply. Do not use the form for urgent matters or to send confidential client, employee or financial records.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers who help us operate the enquiry process, or disclose it where required or permitted by law. We will limit access to people who need the information to respond to the enquiry or perform an authorised business function.

6. Security and retention

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the nature of the information to protect it against loss, unauthorised access, use, alteration or disclosure. Enquiry information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential business relationship, meet legal requirements or resolve disputes, and then securely deleted or de-identified where appropriate. A formal retention schedule must be adopted before launch.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, object to certain processing, or raise a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa. To make a request, email shesparkle0@gmail.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request.

8. Cookies and analytics

This draft website does not intentionally use advertising cookies or analytics tracking. Embedded third-party services, hosting providers or future additions may have their own technical logs or cookies. This notice should be updated if those features are introduced.

9. Changes to this notice

We may update this notice as our services and legal obligations develop. The latest version will be made available on this website.
