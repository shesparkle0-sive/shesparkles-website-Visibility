Who we are

Professional support for the work behind your business.

SheSparkles Business Services is a developing South African business-support practice focused on helping SMMEs, sole proprietors and owner-managed businesses manage routine financial administration.

We take a practical, structured approach: understand your workload, agree on the scope, document responsibilities and deliver the support your business needs.

  • Workload-based service arrangements
  • Confidential handling of business information
  • Clear deliverables, timelines and approvals
  • Remote-first support where suitable
