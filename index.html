About SheSparkles

Good business decisions start with organised financial information.

SheSparkles Business Services is a developing South African business-support practice focused on routine accounting administration, bookkeeping and payroll support for small businesses and owner-managed enterprises.

We believe in a practical and transparent working relationship: understanding your requirements, agreeing on responsibilities and deliverables, and keeping communication clear throughout the engagement.

Our services are scoped according to the work involved—not a one-size-fits-all package.

Tax Administration Support

SheSparkles does not present itself as a registered tax practitioner, auditor or assurance provider. Tax advice, tax practitioner services, audits, attestations and other regulated work will only be undertaken by appropriately authorised professionals.

Bookkeeping & once-off work

Monthly bookkeeping, clean-up projects and once-off assignments are quoted after reviewing the expected transaction volumes, condition of records, reconciliations and reporting requirements.

Software subscriptions, third-party charges, travel and work outside the agreed scope may be quoted separately. No unlimited-work packages.
